# Lab 06: Onboard a Workload

## Intake data

- Business owner and technical owner
- Data classification and compliance requirements
- Production and non-production environments
- Region and recovery requirements
- Corp, Online, or Local connectivity model
- Address-space requirement
- Inbound, outbound, private endpoint, and DNS requirements
- Monitoring and security integration
- Budget and cost center

## Workflow

1. Create or select the workload subscription.
2. Place it under the correct management group.
3. Confirm inherited policy and RBAC.
4. Deploy the workload spoke from its separate workload repository.
5. Request or create approved hub connectivity.
6. Configure routes and DNS.
7. Connect diagnostics and security tooling.
8. Validate traffic, name resolution, compliance, logging, and cost ownership.
9. Hand over documented responsibilities to the workload team.

## Completion criteria

The workload is not onboarded only because its subscription exists. Governance, connectivity, monitoring, security, ownership, and recovery requirements must all be accepted.
