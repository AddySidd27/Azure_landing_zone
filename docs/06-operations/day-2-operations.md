# Day-2 Operations

## Daily

- Review critical security and service-health alerts.
- Review failed platform deployments.
- Investigate unexpected public exposure or privileged access.

## Weekly

- Review policy compliance and remediation failures.
- Review Firewall, DNS, connectivity, and route changes.
- Review platform cost anomalies.
- Review Terraform drift reports.

## Monthly

- Review privileged access and PIM activations.
- Review budgets, shared-service cost allocation, and unused resources.
- Test selected alert receivers and operational queries.
- Review policy exemptions approaching expiry.
- Check provider, module, and ALZ guidance updates.

## Quarterly

- Exercise state recovery and a platform recovery scenario.
- Review architecture decisions against current requirements.
- Review subscription ownership and lifecycle.
- Validate emergency-access procedures.
- Confirm documentation and diagrams still match Azure.

## Change rule

Every platform change should have an owner, reason, impact review, plan evidence, approval, validation result, and rollback or recovery approach.
