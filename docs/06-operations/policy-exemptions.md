# Policy Exemptions

An exemption is a controlled exception, not a permanent bypass.

Record:

- policy assignment;
- resource or subscription scope;
- business justification;
- risk owner;
- mitigation;
- approval;
- start and expiry date;
- review result.

Use the smallest scope and shortest reasonable duration. Review exemptions before expiry. If many workloads need the same exemption, reassess the policy design or platform capability instead of renewing exceptions silently.
