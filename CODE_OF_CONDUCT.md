# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, technical, and constructive.

- Discuss the work, not the person.
- Do not publish credentials, customer data, or private environment details.
- Provide reproducible evidence when reporting a defect.
- Accept that architecture decisions depend on business and technical requirements.
- Do not present this learning repository as an official Microsoft implementation.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, or abusive behavior are not accepted. Repository maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these rules.
